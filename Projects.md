Dear colleague,
If you have ever taken part in a WiCS+ event, you are invited to participate in a research study examining the global Women in Cognitive Science (WiCS+) network and its impact on researchers' careers.
Over the past 25 years, WiCS+ and its regional chapters (WiCS+Europe and WiCSCanada+) have organised numerous events, mentoring activities, networking sessions, workshops, and professional development initiatives. The purpose of this study is to understand the global WiCS+ network, the professional connections it has fostered, and the impact it has had on participants' careers.
To participate, we ask you to complete an online survey. It will take approximately 20 minutes (depending on your responses). We will ask about your involvement in WiCS+ activities and the professional connections you have made through the network.
Please access the survey here: https://test.uib.es/enquestes/index.php/211693?lang=en 
This is a systematic effort to evaluate the impact of WiCS+ since its foundation and to document the professional connections, collaborations, mentoring relationships, and opportunities that have emerged through the network.
To learn more about the project, please visit the study pre-registration: https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23378838
Thank you very much for your time and for helping us better understand and strengthen the WiCS+ community.
